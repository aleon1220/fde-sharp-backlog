Discovery Note - Sprint 14 Planning
Context: Support engineers reported that customers cannot export their monthly audit logs when the record count exceeds 5,000 rows.
Requirement: As an organization admin, I need to export monthly audit logs as a CSV file asynchronously so that compliance reviews do not time out in the browser.
Precondition: An authenticated organization admin is on the Audit Logs page with a date filter selected.
Action: The admin clicks "Export CSV".
Expected Outcome: The system queues an export job and displays a download link within 30 seconds.
